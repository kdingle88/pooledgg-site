How it works

Three steps. Your program stays yours.

  1. Form a group

    Family, friends, coworkers, a community. Anyone who wants to save alongside people they know.

  2. Each member saves in their own account

    Every deposit goes into the member's own credit union account. Nothing is pooled, transferred or shared.

  3. Progress together, rewards together

    The group sees shared progress and unlocks group rewards from the credit union.

  • pooled.gg never holds or moves money
  • Built on your existing program rules
  • Private by default: groups see progress, not balances
